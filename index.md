We collect the SPARQL examples in different formats,
for different projects of the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics where we
have a public SPARQL endpoint.

## Users of the sparql-examples template

Projects that publish their own SPARQL examples from the [sparql-examples template](https://github.com/sib-swiss/sparql-examples-template) are listed on the [template users page](./examples/template-users.md), which also lists the forks of this repository.

## About this work

The SPARQL examples are developed by the [Semantic Web of data focus group](https://www.sib.swiss/community/network/semantic-web-of-data-focus-group) of the SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics. Papers relevant to this work are:

- SIB's RDF Group Members, [The SIB Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics Semantic Web of data](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad902), Nucleic Acids Research, 2023.
- Bolleman J. et al., [A large collection of bioinformatics question-query pairs over federated knowledge graphs: methodology and applications](https://doi.org/10.1093/gigascience/giaf045), GigaScience, 2025.
